## Note

Selection window: 2026-08-05T13:37 UTC through 2026-08-06T13:37 UTC (09:37 ET to 09:37 ET). All eight WSJ Dow Jones RSS feeds, all ten NYT section RSS feeds, and all eight NBC section RSS feeds listed in the source instructions returned HTTP 200 and were used to build each outlet's candidate pool (366, 343, and 180 raw items respectively); items were filtered on each item's own `pubDate`, not on feed-retrieval time, leaving 113 WSJ, 185 NYT, and 47 NBC in-window candidates, which fell to 96, 121, and 32 respectively after deduplication by canonical URL.

Two access limitations affected verification and, for AP, link selection. First, wsj.com and nytimes.com article pages were not fetched directly — both are known to block or challenge non-browser requests from this environment (WSJ typically returns a bot-detection page; NYT returns HTTP 403) — so WSJ and NYT selections rely on their own RSS `title`/`link`/`pubDate` fields, consistent with prior editions of this digest. Two WSJ items (the Fauci-contempt and FDA-vaccine entries) carried sentence-style, non-headline RSS titles; in both cases a proper headline was reconstructed directly from the article's own URL slug rather than fabricated. Second, checking apnews.com's robots.txt directly this run confirmed it explicitly disallows Claude-family crawlers (`ClaudeBot`, `anthropic-ai`, `Claude-Web`, and others), so no apnews.com pages were fetched. AP does not publish a general-news RSS feed, so its 100-item candidate pool was built from Google News's `site:apnews.com when:1d` search feed (all 100 items fell inside the window per Google's reported timestamps, so none were excluded by the time filter). Rather than link to apnews.com or an unresolved `news.google.com/rss/articles/...` redirect (which requires a client-side call a scripted fetch cannot cleanly resolve), each AP-sourced headline below links to an independent wire-copy mirror (Yahoo News, KOB-TV, WRAL, a Lee Enterprises regional paper, and AOL), each confirmed via direct fetch to carry an explicit Associated Press byline and matching headline and details. This is a deliberate substitution, not a fabricated or guessed URL. One of those five, the Fauci-contempt vote, was still actively updating at collection time: AP's Google News-indexed copy and several early mirrors still read in future tense ("Senate panel to vote...") even after the vote had already happened, per confirming WSJ/NYT/NBC/CNN reporting; the Yahoo News mirror used here was verified by direct fetch to carry AP's updated, past-tense wire text.
