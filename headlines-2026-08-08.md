Two access limitations affected sourcing. First, wsj.com and nytimes.com article pages were not fetched directly this run — both returned fetch errors consistent with bot detection — so WSJ selections rely on the outlet's own RSS `title`/`link`/`pubDate` fields; two WSJ items (the ballroom-ruling and Blanche-confirmation entries) carried sentence-style, non-headline RSS titles, and in both cases a proper headline was reconstructed directly from the article's own URL slug rather than fabricated.
