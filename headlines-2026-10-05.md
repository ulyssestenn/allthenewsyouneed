## Note

**Selection window:** 2026-10-04 13:37 UTC to 2026-10-05 13:37 UTC (the 24 hours preceding compilation), filtered on each feed's original `pubDate`/`published` timestamp, not an update or retrieval time.

**Access limitations:**
- `apnews.com` returns an HTTP 403 bot challenge to direct fetches in this environment, and AP publishes no general-purpose RSS feed, so candidates were sourced via Google News search (`site:apnews.com when:1d`) as instructed. Decoding the Google News redirect IDs to `apnews.com`'s own canonical URLs via Google's internal `batchexecute` article-resolution endpoint was attempted but the RPC call was rejected by Google's backend from this environment (likely session/signature validation tied to the requesting IP). As a fallback, each AP candidate's headline, original publication timestamp, and content were independently cross-verified against a verbatim AP-wire syndication copy (identical byline/body, carried under an `ap-top-news` or `wireStory` path by an AP member station or partner site), and that syndication copy's URL is linked in place of the unreachable `apnews.com` original. No URL was fabricated or left unresolved; every AP link above was fetched and its `datePublished` metadata confirmed to fall inside the 24-hour window.
- `wsj.com` blocks direct fetches with a DataDome bot challenge (HTTP 401) in this environment, so WSJ headlines/timestamps were taken as published in Dow Jones' own RSS feeds rather than independently re-verified against the live page; tracking query parameters (`?mod=...`) were stripped from feed links to produce canonical article URLs, consistent with prior digests in this repository.
- `nytimes.com` and `nbcnews.com`/`today.com` fetches were not independently re-verified beyond their own official RSS feeds, which is treated as sufficient cross-verification since the links and timestamps originate directly from the outlets' own feed metadata.

**Principal selection rationale:** Prioritized developments with durable, 30-days-from-now consequence over the day's volume of midterm-campaign theater, Cornell-scandal follow-ups, and NFL recaps: the emergency withdrawal of U.S. B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford after a suspected Iran-linked terror plot against a NATO-ally base; the Supreme Court's new term opening with a major climate-liability case against energy companies; snap elections called in Spain after a government defeat over its housing-affordability decrees; a tight, hemisphere-reshaping Brazil presidential runoff between Lula and a Trump-aligned Bolsonaro; a resurgence of attacks on Gulf shipping and Russian oil-refining capacity that is reshaping global energy markets; a $22.6 billion industrial-software acquisition (Schneider Electric/PTC); an FBI counterintelligence arrest tied to alleged Chinese surveillance of Taiwan's president's family; a new White House-coordinated federal AI task force ("Super Intelligence Force"); continued Russian strikes on Kyiv during a foreign head-of-government visit; and OPEC+'s decision to hold oil output steady amid Middle East-driven price pressure. Deprioritized: Cornell rape-case and "tipster" follow-ups, NFL injury/game recaps, box-office and awards coverage, Prime Day shopping content, and routine opinion columns.
