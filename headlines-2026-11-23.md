## Note

**Window:** Stories originally published between 2026-11-22 14:39 UTC and 2026-11-23 14:39 UTC.

**Access limitations:**
- Direct fetches of `apnews.com` returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare bot block), consistent with prior days. AP has no general-purpose RSS feed, so candidates were sourced via a Google News search scoped to `site:apnews.com` with a 1-day window, as specified.
- Google News wraps AP links in opaque, client-side-rendered redirect URLs (`news.google.com/rss/articles/...`) that no longer resolve via a simple HTTP redirect or static-HTML parse; the decode endpoint Google News' own front end uses to resolve them is an internal, unauthenticated API and this session's tooling would not call it. Canonical `apnews.com` URLs were instead cross-verified one at a time via targeted search-engine lookups (matching each candidate headline's distinctive proper nouns against indexed `apnews.com/article/...` results), a slow, individually-unreliable method: most attempts for secondary AP candidates (an appeals-court ruling on pregnant-workers protections, the original Benghazi warship-strike dispatch, an Nvidia-adjacent market-rebound piece, science-funding, Indonesia/Nepal/CVS/SpaceX items) returned generic, unrelated results and could not be confirmed. Only four AP candidates could be matched to a confirmed canonical URL in the time available.
- Per the instructions not to pad the list with unverifiable items, AP is listed with four headlines rather than five. The strongest AP candidates whose canonical URLs could not be confirmed are recorded below.
- WSJ, NYT, and NBC links come directly from each outlet's own RSS feeds (no redirect resolution needed) and are canonical as published.
