## Note

**Selection window:** 2026-10-25 13:40 UTC to 2026-10-26 13:40 UTC (original publication time, not update or crawl time).

**Access and verification:** All eight WSJ feeds, ten NYT feeds, and eight NBC feeds listed in the sourcing instructions were fetched directly and parsed successfully. AP does not run a general-purpose RSS feed, so candidates came from the Google News search feed (`site:apnews.com when:1d`) as instructed. Each selected AP item's `news.google.com` redirect link was resolved to its canonical `apnews.com/article/...` URL by decoding Google News' signed-URL scheme directly (extracting the article's signature/timestamp from the News page and resolving them through Google's own URL-resolution endpoint) — not by fabrication or guesswork. Direct `curl` fetches of the resulting `apnews.com` URLs returned HTTP 403 (AP's bot defenses), so headline text and timing were cross-verified against the Google News feed's own title and publish-time metadata rather than the live page.

**Principal selection rationale:** Two developments dominated the candidate pool across all four outlets — Brazil's presidential election (Lula's narrow fourth-term win over Flávio Bolsonaro, who disputed the result) and Serbia's election (Aleksandar Vučić conceding to a two-year, student-led protest movement). Both pass the 30-day test: one resets the balance of power in Latin America and U.S.-Brazil relations, the other ends a decade-plus strongman tenure via sustained civic mobilization. Beyond those, selections leaned toward durable business/technology shifts (Anthropic's IPO filing and 14-fold growth disclosure; Meta's and SpaceX's multibillion-dollar AI financing raises; the hardening of competing U.S.- and China-led AI blocs), active war/diplomacy (the Houthi strike on Saudi-massed troops, Iran war-renewal diplomacy, Kim Jong Un's China trip), and live U.S. election-law disputes (the DOJ's Alaska certification suit, the Ninth Circuit's skepticism of DOJ's California ballot challenge). Israel's national election was excluded from every outlet's list on purpose: multiple outlets covered it heavily, but the vote itself is scheduled for October 27 — a preview of a scheduled event rather than a development that has happened, per the selection standard's explicit preference.

**Feed limitations:** None of the 27 requested feeds failed outright. The NBC candidate pool was comparatively thin (30 unique in-window items after dedup, versus 60–80 for the other outlets), limiting the pool of qualifying non-sports, non-lifestyle items.
