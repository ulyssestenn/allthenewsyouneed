## Note

**Selection window:** Stories originally published between 2026-11-15 13:40 UTC and 2026-11-16 13:40 UTC, verified against each feed's `pubDate` (not update time or retrieval time).

**Access and feed limitations:** All eight WSJ (Dow Jones) feeds, all ten NYT feeds, and all eight NBC feeds were reachable directly and returned current content; NBC's world and science feeds had no items published inside the 24-hour window. Direct requests to the five selected WSJ article pages returned HTTP 401 to this session's scripted request (WSJ enforces bot/subscription gating on article pages even though its RSS feeds are open); each URL nonetheless comes verbatim from WSJ's own official RSS feed and is the canonical article link. AP has no general-purpose public RSS feed, so candidates were sourced via Google News' `site:apnews.com when:1d` search feed, as instructed, after excluding non-article AP topic/index pages (e.g., "Iran war," "Latest news," sports scoreboards, state election-results trackers) that the Google News feed mixes in alongside genuine articles. Direct requests to apnews.com returned a Cloudflare bot-verification challenge (HTTP 403), and this session's web-search tool is blocked from crawling apnews.com directly, so the canonical apnews.com URLs could not be resolved or verified by domain-restricted search. As in recent digests, the five AP links above point to the Google News article pages for these stories (each confirmed HTTP 200 and matched against the AP headline, snippet, and timestamp) rather than resolved apnews.com URLs; each leads to the correct canonical AP article when opened in a standard browser.

**Selection rationale:** Two developments dominated the raw candidate pool — the continuing fallout from the FAA's multi-day nationwide ground stop (now reaching funding fights in Congress, a defense-contractor accountability angle, and an NTSB finding on an unrelated New Orleans tanker collision) and the Jensen Huang/Nvidia scandal, which has metastasized into a "national champions" AI-policy question, an insurance-industry AI-debt exposure story, and speculation about Huang's own future. Rather than let either story crowd out the rest of the day's news, each outlet's five selections include at most two headlines tied to either story, chosen for distinct durable angles (e.g., infrastructure funding vs. safety-incident accountability; AI industrial policy vs. financial-sector risk) rather than restating the same development. Stories selected mainly for virality or novelty — Black Friday deal skepticism, celebrity court statements, NFL/NBA recaps, awards-season chatter, and Trump's Qatari-jet travel arrangements — were deprioritized in favor of stories with clearer 30-day institutional, economic, or geopolitical consequence. Trump's three-way Asia summit with Xi and Putin, while widely covered, was included only once per outlet (where at all) because it is still substantially a preview of a scheduled event rather than a conclusized development.
