**Access and verification:** All WSJ (Dow Jones), NYT, and NBC RSS feeds listed in the sourcing instructions were reachable directly. AP has no general RSS feed; AP candidates were sourced via the Google News `site:apnews.com when:1d` search feed, then each selected story's canonical `apnews.com` URL was resolved from Google's redirect wrapper using a headless-browser fetch (Google's redirect requires JS execution; a direct HTTP fetch of the wrapped link returns a 302 back to itself rather than the publisher URL).

**Principal selection rationale:** The dominant story of the cycle across all four outlets was Nvidia's board reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO after President Trump personally intervened — a corporate-governance event with direct bearing on AI-sector market stability, congressional national-security scrutiny, and a precedent for executive-branch pressure on private boards. Each outlet includes at most one or two angles of this story (never three or more), each representing a genuinely distinct consequence (the reinstatement itself vs. the political/congressional backlash vs., for WSJ/AP/NBC, the separate but same-day federal appeals court ruling opening a path to move/erase Trump's hush-money conviction, which is an unrelated legal development). Beyond that story, selections favor concrete, resolved developments (a completed $41B pharma acquisition, a completed $44B cybersecurity merger, an elite CCP corruption investigation, a verified war-casualty analysis, a diplomatic rift over the ICC, a public-health status change) over previews, proposals, or developing/unconfirmed situations.
