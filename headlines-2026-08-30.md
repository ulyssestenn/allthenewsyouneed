**Selection window:** 2026-08-29 13:36 UTC to 2026-08-30 13:36 UTC (24 hours), filtered by original publication timestamp (each outlet's RSS `<pubDate>`/`published`, or, for AP, each candidate's own `publication_date` from AP's public Google News-format sitemap), not update or retrieval time.

**Access notes:**
- All eight listed WSJ feeds, all ten listed NYT feeds, and all eight listed NBC feeds were fetched directly and parsed successfully. No domain or feed was inaccessible.
- AP does not publish a general RSS feed. The Google News search (`site:apnews.com when:1d`) was fetched for candidate discovery, but its links are opaque `news.google.com/rss/articles/...` redirect tokens that do not resolve to `apnews.com` via direct HTTP request (the redirect is JavaScript-driven). AP's public Google News-format sitemap (`apnews.com/news-sitemap-content.xml`) was used instead to independently discover and verify AP's candidate pool, each with its own `publication_date` and canonical `apnews.com/article/...` URL, cross-checked against the Google News search results for topical coverage.
- Two WSJ social-feed items (`socialpoliticsfeed`) — a Social Security insolvency/2032-deadline story and a Milo Yiannopoulos deportation story — carry a teaser sentence in the RSS `<title>` field rather than the article's actual headline. The Social Security piece's real headline could not be independently verified via web search in the time available, so it was excluded from WSJ's five rather than risk an inaccurate headline; the Yiannopoulos item was independently confirmed via WSJ's own `usnews` feed (real headline: "ICE Deports British Media Provocateur Milo Yiannopoulos") but was not selected, per the personality-driven deprioritization rule.
- Three candidate URLs that appeared in today's feeds were excluded as duplicates of stories already used in yesterday's (2026-08-29) digest, where the outlet appears to have updated an existing article's feed timestamp rather than publishing a new one: NYT's Kyiv-strike piece (`russia-ukraine-strike-kyiv-drones.html`), NYT's Nepal flood-rescue piece (`nepal-flood-rescue.html`), NYT's Stanford-students ruling (`trump-stanford-students-deport-constitution.html`), and NYT's original Venezuela-oil-deal piece (`2026/08/28/business/trump-venezuela-oil-deal.html`).
