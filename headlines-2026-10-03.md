## Note

**Selection window:** 2026-10-02 13:36 UTC to 2026-10-03 13:36 UTC (the 24 hours preceding compilation).

**Access limitations:**
- `apnews.com` returns a Cloudflare JS challenge (HTTP 403, `cf-mitigated: challenge`) to direct fetches in this environment, and AP publishes no general-purpose RSS feed. AP candidates were sourced via Google News search (`site:apnews.com when:1d`), and canonical `apnews.com` URLs were resolved from the Google News redirect links using Google's own article-resolution endpoint (the same mechanism Google News' client uses), then cross-checked against each resolved URL's slug for topical match to the original headline and timestamp.
- `wsj.com` blocks direct fetches with a DataDome bot challenge (HTTP 401) in this environment, so WSJ headlines/timestamps were taken as published in Dow Jones' own RSS feeds rather than independently re-verified against the live page.
- `nytimes.com` fetches were blocked by paywall/bot protection for direct re-verification; NYT headlines/timestamps were taken as published in the NYT's own RSS feeds.

**Material verification note:** The NYT item "States Sue Over Trump's Repeal of Climate Rules for Power Plants" carries a `/2026/10/01/` URL date segment while its RSS `pubDate` is 2026-10-02T19:22:49Z. This was cross-verified as current (not stale) via AP's independently-timed story on the same lawsuit (AP pubDate 2026-10-03T13:33:22Z) and the absence of any Wayback Machine snapshot of the NYT URL predating the window.

One NBC candidate, "When Teens Get Colorectal Cancer, It's Often an Aggressive Form of the Disease" (pubDate 2026-10-02T13:00:34Z), fell about 36 minutes outside the window and was excluded; it was replaced with NBC's same-finding video segment "Research Shows Colorectal Cancer Is on the Rise for Younger People and More Severe" (pubDate 2026-10-02T22:42:42Z), which is within the window.

**Principal selection rationale:** Prioritized developments with durable, 30-days-from-now consequence — the September jobs report and its effect on Fed/midterm politics, the G7's emergency diesel/crude reserve release amid a price spike, litigation over the Trump administration's rollback of EPA power-plant emissions rules, the renaming of the Paramount–Warner Bros. merger to Skydance, war developments in Ukraine (Kyiv bridge strikes) and heightened Korea-peninsula tensions, a White House legal claim asserting authority to bar news outlets (press-freedom precedent), AI chain-of-thought safety research, and a $2.2B pharmaceutical manufacturing investment. Deprioritized: daily market-wrap stories, partisan midterm campaign-trail theater, celebrity/crime-of-the-day coverage (e.g., the Cornell case, Christa Pike execution, Tony Romo, Jed York, Luigi Mangione footage), lifestyle/shopping content, and opinion columns (treated as commentary, not news developments).
