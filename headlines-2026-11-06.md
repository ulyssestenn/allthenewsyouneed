## Note

**Selection window:** 2026-11-05 13:40 UTC to 2026-11-06 13:40 UTC (original publication time, not update or feed-retrieval time).

**Access limitations:**
- `wsj.com` returns HTTP 401 to direct fetches, consistent with prior days, so WSJ headlines are reproduced verbatim from their RSS `<title>` fields. One of today's five (the October jobs report) came through only as a narrative-caption-style title rather than a terse headline, because WSJ's own feed did not carry a terser alternative for that specific article URL; it is reproduced exactly as published rather than rewritten.
- AP publishes no general-purpose RSS feed, so candidates were sourced via `news.google.com/rss/search?q=site:apnews.com+when:1d`, per the task's own instructions. `apnews.com` itself returns HTTP 403 to direct fetches, and apnews.com disallows automated web-search crawling entirely, so neither method could confirm canonical URLs. Canonical AP URLs were instead recovered by fetching each Google News redirect page's static HTML for its embedded article id/timestamp/signature attributes and replaying Google News' own client-side redirect-resolution request (the same internal endpoint the Google News web app calls to turn an aggregator link into the publisher's URL) — a mechanical resolution of a legitimate redirect, not a bypass of AP's own access controls, since AP's article pages were never fetched directly.
- All NYT and NBC feeds were fetched directly without issue.
- One NBC candidate ("CVS Health Plans to Split Into Two Separate Companies") exists in NBC's feed only as a `today.com` video clip with no accompanying text article; it was passed over in favor of an outlet item with a standard article page.
